Quick & Effortless

3 Simple Steps for a Deeper Clean

Follow our tested 3-step routine to maintain spotless, hygienically clean toilets with zero effort.

01

Apply

Press the child-resistant safety cap and twist open. Direct the angled precision nozzle under the toilet bowl rim and squeeze evenly around the bowl.

💡 Tip: Ensure the thick gel coats the inner rim where stains originate.
02

Leave

Allow the thick clinging formula to work for 20 minutes. The active descaling ingredients break down stubborn yellow marks, uric scale, and bacteria.

💡 Tip: For heavily calcified stains, leave overnight for best results.
03

Clean & Flush

Brush lightly above and below the water line using a toilet brush. Flush for a fresh, gleaming, and hygienically clean toilet.

💡 Tip: Enjoy long-lasting ocean freshness with every flush.
⚠️
Safety Direction: Use only for toilet bowls. Keep out of reach of children. Do not mix with bleach or other household chemicals. Store in a cool, dry place.

Our Story

Powerful Cleaning for Everyday Life

DEEPKIL was created with a clear purpose: to make everyday toilet cleaning easier, more effective, and more hygienic for Indian households.

Across India, municipal and borewell water contains elevated levels of mineral hardness and dissolved solids. Ordinary thin cleaners run straight into the water trap without having time to dissolve stains, forcing homemakers to resort to harsh corrosive acids that ruin ceramic glaze and produce choking fumes.

DEEPKIL bridges this gap with an advanced, high-viscosity clinging formulation that adheres to vertical bowl surfaces, gently yet decisively breaking down hard water rings, stubborn yellow marks, and foul odors.
